Day 8> Sep. 4

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 10-11
10:3-5 "For though we live in the world, we do not wage war as the world does. The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world. On the contrary, they have divine power to demolish strongholds. We demolish arguments and every pretension that sets itself up against the knowledge of God, and we take captive every thought to make it obedient to Christ."
We do not live as the world does. Although we live in the world, we as Christians are citizens of the kingdom on Heaven, and we are to live as ambassadors of that kingdom. This passage says that we are to do that by controling even our thoughts, not just spoken words or actions.

Day 7. Sep. 3

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 5-9
9:13b-15 "others will praise God for the obedience that accompanies your cofession of the gospel of Christ... And in their prayer for you their hearts will go out to you, because of the surpassing grace God has given you. Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!"
Being obedient to the Gospel of Christ is one way to shine God's light to others, both Christians and those who are not Christians. God gifts us ways to show His love to others.

Day 6. Sep. 2

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 2-5
5:19,21 "God was reconciling the world to himself in Christ, not counting people's sins against them... God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God."
Jesus Christ, who had no sin, took on my sin and your sin and was punished for our sin so that we can be reconciled with God. Because Jesus paid the price, He offers a free gift to you, a gift no one else can offer. This gift is forgiveness of sins and a right relationship with God. God is holy and perfect, so no sin can enter into His presence. Jesus offers to take away your sin so that you can enter into God's presence. Day 5: Sep. 1


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 16 and 2 Corinthians 1
2 Corinthians 1:3-5 "Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our troubles so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves recieve from God. For just as we share abundantly in the sufferings of Christ, so also our comfort abounds through Christ."

Day 4: Aug. 31


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 13-15
15:1-6 "I want to remind you of the gospel I preached to you... By this gospel you are saved... that Christ died for our sins... that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day... and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve... [and then] to more than five hundred of the brothers and sisters at the same time."
These are the important aspects of our faith in Jesus Christ, which are important to remember. Christ died, was buried, rose on the third day, and appeared to many others. The "more than five hundred... at the same time" is important to note because some people who don't believe say that the apostles hallucinated or imagined Jesus alive again. There is no such thing as mass hallucination - Jesus appeared to these 500 people in real life.

Day 3: Aug. 30


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 11-12
12:18 "But in fact God has placed the parts in the body, every one of them, just as He wanted them to be."
The parts in the body are the people in the church family. God has placed every person in the situation they are in for a reason. Every person has a plan and a purpose in God.

Day 2: Aug. 29

Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 8-10.
One thing that stood out to me was 9:20-23 where Paul says that he became like the people he was trying to reach.
"To the Jews I became like a Jew, to win the Jews." v 20.

This shows an important thing to keep in mind when witnessing about Jesus to people of other religions: learn about them, understand where they are coming from and use that to firstly, have them actually listen to what you have to say and secondly, to tell in a way that makes sense to them and is convincing from their point of view.
