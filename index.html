Day 15: Sep. 11

Today's Bible reading was Phillippians 3-4
4:4-7 "Rejoice in the Lord always. I will say it again: Rejoice! Let your gentleness be evident to all. The Lord is near. Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."
No matter the situation around you, good, bad, or in between, Paul tells up to rejoice. Have joy, one of the fruit of the Spirit, no matter your circumstances. And if you are anxious, present your requests to God with thanksgiving. Find something to praise God for, even in your worry. Like 1 Peter 5:7 says,
"Cast all your anxiety on him [God] because he cares for you.
It will be hard. But keep trying. Lean on Him, and He will be there.

Day 12: Sep. 8

Day 11: Sep 7

Today's Bible reading was Galatians 3-6
5:13-15 "You, my brothers and sisters, were called to be free. But do not use your freedom to indulge the flesh; rather, serve one another humbly in love. For the entire law is fulfilled in this command: 'Love your neighbor as yourself.' If you bite and devour each other, watch out or you will be destroyed by each other."

Day 10: Sep. 6

Today's Bible reading was Galatians 1-3
Galatians 3:15-22 is about the law, the old covenant, and the new covenant. I highly encourage you to open your Bible and read the whole thing yourself, I quote only parts here.
"The promises were spoken to Abraham and to his seed... [the seed] meaning one person, who is Christ... The law... does not set aside the coveneant previously established by God and thus do away with the promise. For if the inheritance depends on the law, then it no longer depends on the promise, but God in his grace gave it to Abraham through a promise."
This passage is important to think about because of the Hebrew Roots movement that says we as Christians are required to keep the old law, festivals, customs, etc.

Day 9. Sep. 5

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 12-13
13:5 "Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Do you not realize that Christ Jesus is in you -- unless, of course, you fail the test?"
In this verse, Paul is telling us to be careful that we are not decieved into thinking that we are Christians when we actually are not. This is a warning to all believers: make certain that you are truly a Christian.

Day 8> Sep. 4

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 10-11
10:3-5 "For though we live in the world, we do not wage war as the world does. The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world. On the contrary, they have divine power to demolish strongholds. We demolish arguments and every pretension that sets itself up against the knowledge of God, and we take captive every thought to make it obedient to Christ."
We do not live as the world does. Although we live in the world, we as Christians are citizens of the kingdom on Heaven, and we are to live as ambassadors of that kingdom. This passage says that we are to do that by controling even our thoughts, not just spoken words or actions.

Day 6. Sep. 2

Today's Bible reading was 2 Corinthians 2-5
5:19,21 "God was reconciling the world to himself in Christ, not counting people's sins against them... God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God."
Jesus Christ, who had no sin, took on my sin and your sin and was punished for our sin so that we can be reconciled with God. Because Jesus paid the price, He offers a free gift to you, a gift no one else can offer. This gift is forgiveness of sins and a right relationship with God. God is holy and perfect, so no sin can enter into His presence. Jesus offers to take away your sin so that you can enter into God's presence. Day 4: Aug. 31


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 13-15
15:1-6 "I want to remind you of the gospel I preached to you... By this gospel you are saved... that Christ died for our sins... that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day... and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve... [and then] to more than five hundred of the brothers and sisters at the same time."
These are the important aspects of our faith in Jesus Christ, which are important to remember. Christ died, was buried, rose on the third day, and appeared to many others. The "more than five hundred... at the same time" is important to note because some people who don't believe say that the apostles hallucinated or imagined Jesus alive again. There is no such thing as mass hallucination - Jesus appeared to these 500 people in real life.

Day 2: Aug. 29

Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 8-10.
One thing that stood out to me was 9:20-23 where Paul says that he became like the people he was trying to reach.
"To the Jews I became like a Jew, to win the Jews." v 20.

This shows an important thing to keep in mind when witnessing about Jesus to people of other religions: learn about them, understand where they are coming from and use that to firstly, have them actually listen to what you have to say and secondly, to tell in a way that makes sense to them and is convincing from their point of view.
