Day 4: Aug. 31


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 13-15
15:1-6 "I want to remind you of the gospel I preached to you... By this gospel you are saved... that Christ died for our sins... that he was buried, that he waa raised on the third day... and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve... [and then] to more than five hundred of the brothers and sisters at the same time."
These are the important aspects of our faith in Jesus Christ, which are important to remember. Christ died, was buried, rose on the third day, and appeared to many others. The "more than five hundred... at the same time" is important to note because some people who don't believe say that the apostles hallucinated or imagined Jesus alive again. There is no such thing as mass hallucination - Jesus appeared to these 500 people in real life.

Day 3: Aug. 30


Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 11-12
12:18 "But in fact God has placed the parts in the body, every one of them, just as He wanted them to be." The parts in the body are the people in the church family. God has placed every person in the situation they are in for a reason. Every person has a plan and a purpose in God.

Day 2: Aug. 29

Today's Bible reading was 1 Corinthians 8-10.
One thing that stood out to me was 9:20-23 where Paul says that he became like the people he was trying to reach. "To the Jews I became like a Jew, to win the Jews." v 20.
This shows an important thing to keep in mind when witnessing about Jesus to people of other religions: learn about them, understand where they are coming from and use that to firstly, have them actually listen to what you have to say and secondly, to tell in a way that makes sense to them and is convincing from their point of view.
